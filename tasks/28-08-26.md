### Rigging

**Arms and hands are broken on every model, in cutscenes and in gameplay.**

Six hypotheses were killed by measurement before the cause was found; do not
re-run them:

| theory | killed by |
|---|---|
| bind-pose mismatch, tiny deltas | `left_shoulder` carries 81.7 deg |
| wrong quaternion axis conversion | `(x,y,z)->(x,-z,y)` is det=+1, correct as `gani_to_anim.py` documents |
| finger joints stealing forearm weights | hdg_2411's weights are clean |
| stale `offsets` during choreography | `SetOffset` is only ever called with bias 0.0, which erases; `CalculateGeomOffsets` is commented out |
| `smooth`/`smoothFactor` in the choreo path | `if (smooth && 1 == 2)` is dead code |
| clavicle swinging the arm chain | displaces the hand 0.018 m |

What is established: `fmdl_to_fullbody.nearest_joints` binds hand geometry to
**finger joints** - measured at 0.033 m on `lcg_2709`, nearer than the wrist -
and `HandRig::Apply` curls those 38 joints every frame toward poses like
`open_full_ball`. A palm-sized chunk of mesh rides on a pinky. `joint_binding.py
--all` reports 34 models with a dead arm joint and nine with no arm chain at
all.

The fix is **not** to fold finger influence into the wrist. That is the same
decimation this file exists to forbid; the engine must carry PES's hand rig
1:1. Next action: read PES's own per-vertex skin weights where the fmdl carries
them, and find why hand vertices reach the `nearest_joints` fallback at all
(`vertex_joints`, `fmdl_to_fullbody.py:68`).

**Celebrations snap players supine mid-air.** `SetChoreoPose` reads
`position.Get2D()`, discarding Z, so a choreography pose cannot lift an actor
off the ground - but the supine snap comes from the celebration clip's own root
motion via `e_FunctionType_Special` selection, not from choreography.
`match.cpp:1989-1994` documents the same failure mode being hit before.

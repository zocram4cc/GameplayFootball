## Fixed: Wario's dragging verts (owner priority 3)

Measured on the installed `smg_2579` weights: **419 body vertices had a dominant finger
joint**, and 81 triangles spanned joints > 0.6 m apart (hip-to-pinky_dip, knee-to-thumb_pip).
Cause: `rebind_stray` moved any vertex whose mapped joint was > 0.15 m away to the *nearest*
joint of any kind. Wario is 2.46 m tall with a belly 0.44 m out: a hip vertex sits 0.58 m
from the hip it is mapped to and 0.57 m from a fingertip beside it, so "closer" sent it to
the finger, which `HandRig` curls and the arm swings every frame.

Fix (`fmdl_to_fullbody.py`): the replacement joint must be a **body** joint
(`body_joint_positions`, ids < 20) and **clearly closer** (`STRAY_RATIO` 0.5). A shin mapped
to the foot (0.02 m from the knee vs 0.38) is still rebound. Re-imported SMBG: finger-dominant
body vertices 419 -> 0. Posed on `dml_goal_celebrate_0001` frame 60 (`pose_render.py`):
`wario2_pair.png` - before, the torso shreds into sheets trailing the raised hand; after, it
stays on the hips. Not yet seen in an engine cutscene capture (no idle window left); that is
the remaining verification.

Where 19-09 ended: real keepers in, and a team-sheet fix from `squad_order`

## What the day proved

1. **The real sheet is the game plan's lineup, and it is documented.** The
   wiki's Texport page maps the Team Game Plan at 0x410 (0x3e0 in the .ted
   plaintext): Preset 1's eleven position codes + eleven (v, h) coordinates,
   then 40 index ids at +0x1e4 - keeper, ten outfield, bench. Checked on all
   seven exports: lineup[0] is the keeper on every one, his coordinate the
   wiki's (3, 0x34) on every one, and the eleven codes agree with the eleven
   registered positions 77 of 77. No pinning: seat 0 needs no keeper guard,
   it arrives as GK.
2. **The registered-position reader is now pinned at the wiki's offsets.**
   The playable-CB slot was misplaced at CM's 0x2a:7; 43 of 161 then decoded
   CB without CB graded A. Fixed to 0x29:7: 161/161 players now grade A at
   their own registered position. Zero LW/RW registered anywhere and zero B
   grades anywhere are real 4cc data, not decoder gaps.
3. **The importer writes PES's sheet, not a seat pattern.** `ted` yields
   `game_plan` (lineup, position tokens, coordinates); `install`/`reseat`
   seat the lineup order directly, roles from the plan's tokens (registered
   position for the bench, seat pattern only where a record names none),
   familiarity from the playable 0/1/2 list through the flat baseline too,
   and `formation_xml` from PES's grid points on the engine's pitch:
   vertical 3 -> -1.0 (keeperX), 47 (the exports' CF line) -> 0.7
   (attackX), horizontal 0x34 centre with full backs at ±36 -> ±0.9. Left
   in engine terms: formation pN matches seat N-1, the keeper's p1 always
   (-1.00, 0.00), full backs at the touchline the editor's own grid puts
   them at.
4. **The engine now speaks all thirteen of PES's positions.** `e_PlayerRole`
   appends `LW, RW, SS` after CF (values 10-12; nothing persists a role as a
   number). Role switches with no `default` keep CF falling into the forward
   branch, so ten-role data still behaves. New consumers: mindset 1.0;
   `SlotFor` LW->LWF/RW->RWF/SS->SS; `InferRatings` A + B/CF-adjacent B;
   `SuitsRole` TargetMan/DummyRunner +SS; `IsWide` +LW/RW, `IsAttacker`
   +LW/RW/SS; strategy dispatch LW/RW midfield, SS strikers; counter-attack
   width/stay-high; menu ten roles -> thirteen; forward-zone + plan lines.
   `GetDefaultRolePosition` only fills the seats no game plan names - the
   importer writes PES's own coordinates where it has them.
5. **All seven re-applied, 7/7 orders == the game plan.** Reseat rewrites
   WHERE (formationorder, role, profile, formation_xml), matched by name so
   row ids never move. /2hug/ lives in the database as `2HUG` (no slashes),
   which is why one verification probe reported it missing - the reseat hit
   the right row, the probe asked for the wrong name.

## Killed

- The `squad_order` reading as the team sheet (keeper at slot 1/9/10) -
  inverted permutation of a byte one past the real lineup's end.
- The invented `ROLE_POSITIONS` table for the formation (engine defaults as
  database values) - the importer writes PES's authored coordinates, and an
  export without a game plan leaves formation_xml empty for the engine's own
  4-4-2 fallback.
- The keeper-pinning in `install`/`reseat` - a guard for the misread, not
  for PES, whose game plans seat the keeper first anyway.

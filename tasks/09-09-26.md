**Finding.** Each `.fdc` is one *state* of a scene: cameras + actor spawn/yaw/gani + props. What assembles a goal — which run-celebration, which celebration, whether teammates hug (LV2/LV3 by count), whether it goes to the fence / behind the goal / flag / camera, how it finishes — is `dt12_fixdemo_all/common/demo/fixdemo/goal/table_goal.bin` (884 KB), and `table_{ent,foul,change,end,timeup,pk,result,mode}.bin` beside it. A state machine we never read. We play ONE `GOAL_CELEBRATE_NNNN` under a six-beat camera template authored from a video; PES plays
`GOAL_START -> GOAL_RUN_30_*/GOAL_RUN_40_* -> GOAL_CELEBRATE_NNNN (421) -> GOAL_HUGA/HUGB_LV2/LV3 -> GOAL_A_{BEHINDGOAL01-03,OVERBOARD,FLAG_DANCE/GOLF/SNIPER,CAMERAAPPEAL,CONFETTI,SUBSTITUTEHUG,TURNBYAREAF} -> GOAL_C_FINISH_{SUCCESS,FAILURE,STAND_*} -> GOAL_END_`, side branches `GOAL_S_OWNGOAL_{WANDERING,CROUCHING,GK}`, `GOAL_S_OFFSIDE*`, `GOAL_S_GOLAZO*`, `GOAL_S_ASSISTTHANX`.

**Format so far (PES21).** Header 16 u32: `/*` magic; `[3]`=0x349f; `[15]`=19 (name stride); `[8]`=0x3c = start of a 159-entry u32 name-offset index. Sections at 0x1ac9, 0x2a2f, 0x349f, 0xe2c0, 0x100b9, 0x25d10, 0x38b3c (f32, 1000-1600), 0x433f8 (7,3 alternating), **0x50674 = CSR adjacency (`count<<16 | first`)**, **0x659bd = 5-u32 edge records `0x??0a0203, 1, 0, 0, 0x000f0100`**, 0x707e6, 0x722af = 721 state names (396 distinct).

### 8. Goalkeepers: luck out, attributes in (15-09, `44e3256`)
Owner: "they fumble the ball a lot, not able to catch. Is it still luck
dependant? It should be more stats based, and not look as goofy." It was, in
two places, and both are gone.

- Whether he even tried was ONE `random(0,1)` per shot against
  `GetKeeperSaveChance` = sharpness x (0.80 + 0.20 x gk_reflexes), i.e. 0.53
  (hopeless keeper) to 0.66 (perfect) on the stock 0.66 sharpness - so a third
  to a half of every shot went unattempted no matter who was in goal, and the
  same roll gated both the dive (`PlayerController`) and the body-collision
  pass (`IsBeatenKeeper`). Replaced by the geometry of the save
  (`KeeperReachesShot`, pure and unit-tested): the ball's flight gives the time,
  GK Reflexes spends 200-350 ms of it as a human reaction
  (`GetKeeperReactionTime_s` - the old `GetReactionTime_ms` is pad lag, 40-80
  ms, far too short to stand in), GK Coverage is the reach at full stretch
  (0.9-2.4 m) and the diving speed that closes the rest (3.5-5.5 m/s).
- Whether he HELD it compared two normalised difficulties multiplied together
  against 0.45 - 0.25 x gk_catching. A 25 m/s shot scores 0.375 before the
  reaction term even applies, so no genuine shot could clear the bar at ANY
  attribute value: everything was parried. That is the fumbling. Now GK
  Catching is a speed - `GetKeeperCatchSpeed_ms`, 12 m/s at zero to 28 m/s at
  the top, 20 m/s at the stock 0.6 - and anything quicker is parried, with GK
  Clearing still deciding how far it goes.

Measured save rate of the new decision over a grid of real shots (three shot
speeds x four ranges x the width of the goal mouth), by attribute:

|0.0|23%|69%|31%|56%|
|0.6 (stock)|54%|100%|62%|83%|
|1.0|69%|100%|92%|93%|

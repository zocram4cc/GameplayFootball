## The on-target number is the AIM, not the arrival (measured 16-09)

Built a second, independent on-target counter to check the first: instead of
projecting the launch (`humanoid.cpp`, same ballistic formula the arrival band
clamps), sample the ball's own two positions either side of the goal-line plane
each tick and interpolate where it met the plane
(`GameplayTuning::FindGoalLineCrossing`, `Match::Process`). Drag, spin,
deflections and the keeper are all in that number; none of them are in the
projection.

Six seeds, `sim110` - the batch *with* the episode fix, so these are the
trustworthy measured columns (pooled over both teams, 128 shots):

| | value |
|---|---|
| shots | 128 |
| projected on target | 80 = **62% of shots** |
| reached the goal-line plane | 29 = **23% of shots** |
| **crossed inside the frame** | 8 = **6% of shots, 10% of the projection** |
| reached the line with no shot in flight | 6 (0 of them in frame) |
| goals | 7 = 1.17/match |
| keeper saves | 50 |
| `goals <= in frame`, per team-match | **holds in all 12** |

`sim100`'s version of these columns is unusable (its crossing count predates
the episode fix and is inflated by the net); ignore it.

The projection says 62% and the ball reaches the posts 6% of the time. Every
ball that crossed inside the frame became a goal, which is what the invariant
check now confirms rather than assumes. Batch variance is wide - `sim100` and
`sim110` ran the same binary on the same seeds and produced 2.00 and 1.17 goals
per match - so treat a single six-seed batch as a direction, not a figure.

So of ~10 shots a team takes, ~6 reach the plane, ~1 is inside the posts, and
~1 is a goal: **the projected 56-64% is the rate at which shots are *aimed* at
the frame, and the rate at which they get there is about a fifth of that.**
Saves (3.5/team) and the missing 4 shots are the same thing seen from the other
side: the keeper and the defence stop most shots before the line, so the stat
card reads four to five times better than the play looks. Worth stating to the
owner as two numbers, not one, and worth deciding whether the stat card should
report the measured one - PES reports what happened, not what was attempted.

### Two bugs this counter had, both fixed

- **The net re-crosses the plane every tick.** `sim100/s5001` reported 31
  crossings from 9 shots: a ball sitting in the net gets nudged back and forth
  across x = ±pitchHalfW by the netting clamp, and each tick counted. Now one
  crossing per episode; the episode closes when the ball is 2 m back inside.
- **`keeper saves N% of shots on target` divided by the projected column**, so
  it was measuring the projection, not the keeper. Now labelled as such, and
  the measured column stands alone.

Also split the counter in two: a crossing with **no shot in the flight window**
(a pass or a deflection reaching the line, which `CheckForGoal` still scores as
a goal) was being dropped, and `sim100/s5003` showed the cost - 3 goals from 2
counted crossings. Those now go to a `crossings-no-shot` column, which also
makes `goals <= crossings in frame` checkable rather than assumed.

## Two measurement caveats on the on-target number (raised 16-09)

1. **"On target" is now partly true by construction.** The on-target test
   projects the ball with the same formula the arrival band clamps against
   (`z = z0 + vz*t - g*t^2/2`, `humanoid.cpp` on-target block and
   `ShotArrivalVelocityZ`), so for every shot where the band applied the
   vertical term lands in 0..2.5 m because we put it there. Only the lateral
   `y` term is still independent. Quote the corroboration - goals and xG, and
   the keeper's saves - not the percentage on its own, and never as proof that
   the *shots got better* rather than that the *statistic got coupled to the
   fix*. Before the band, the same shots crossed the plane below ground, which
   is a real difference in what happens on the pitch; it is the metric that
   cannot be read alone.
2. **Same seed is not bit-identical.** `sim80/s5003` and `sim90/s5003` - same
   binary (md5 `820d6758`), same config, same seed - agree on shots 11-9, on
   target 7-7 and goals 1-0, but differ on xG (0.98-0.60 vs 0.98-0.67), so the
   shots went to different places. Something in the sim is not seeded. Paired
   seeds therefore narrow the variance; they do not remove it, and the claim
   has to stay at the aggregate (n-match mean) level, not per-seed deltas.

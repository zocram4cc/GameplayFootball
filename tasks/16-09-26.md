## Open, in order

- [ ] **True path length in the strike band** (in flight, `humanoid.cpp`).
  `distanceToGoal` used only the axis-aligned `|dx|` to the goal-line plane;
  from a wide position the ball travels a diagonal and the real path is up to
  30% longer, which understates the flight and aims the band low. Now computed
  along `ballDirection`, with the applied/not-applied flag and the strike's
  horizontal speed logged (`[shotband] hz .. path .. t .. applied ..`).
- [ ] **Why 7 of 23 shots still "short"** (z median -5.5 at the line, far below
  anything a band-clamped trajectory produces). If the band never applied, it is
  the flight-time guard or a sub-8 m/s strike - and a sub-8 strike needs a
  MINIMUM SHOT POWER, not a wider band. Decide from the `[shotband]` log.
- [ ] **Paired 12-match batch at `--seed0 5000`.** The 53% was measured on seeds
  6000-6011 against a 5000 baseline, so it is unpaired; re-run so the only
  difference is the code.

  on-target per team is 14%; real football scores ~30% of on-target shots, and
  xG 1.01 ≈ goals 1.01 per team says the chances are taken as modelled. Measure
  the keeper's save rate on those on-target shots before touching shot
  generation again - if it is ~86%, that is the single lever between 1.58 and
  the 2-4 goal target.

  and 4 on target was the bottleneck, not the save. A reach/dive nerf was
  measured and reverted before it reached a commit.

  band was added.

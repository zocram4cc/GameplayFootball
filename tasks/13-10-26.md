## Killed theories (do not re-derive)

- *"Ghidra headless fails because of stale .class files."* Killed: they are
  inert. One script calling an API removed in Ghidra 12 broke the whole
  directory's OSGi bundle.
- *"The camera's u is a 0..1 fraction and h a 0..100 percentage."* Killed by
  the instruction at 0x1408a1ebf: both are scaled by 100 and divided by 100.
- *"The fox2 magic is the literal `\"\\xf2box5\"`."* Killed: `\x` eats the `b`;
  the literal compiles to `+ox5`. Compare explicit bytes.
- *"The ball spin scale is a .data constant."* Killed: 1.0 on disk, written at
  runtime to 492.181 by a one-time initializer.
- *"Set-piece durations are in Tactics.bin."* Killed: Tactics.bin and
  TacticsFormation.bin are an index and a bitmask; the timer fields' shipped
  values remain unlocated.
- *"Pinned kit values are format invariants."* Killed this morning: they were
  4cc pack content.
- *"The leg number turns 90° clockwise."* Killed: the mode-1 emitter table
  `{1,3,0,2}` is the identity; the clockwise rewrite drew a vanilla 7 upside down.
- *"The stamina tiers sit in an encrypted descriptor table at 0x14161e000."*
  Killed: it is plain code, byte-identical on disk and live; the registry
  behind the tier names is Fox's DataElement class registry.
- *"Stadium.bin +370 is the folder."* Killed: vanilla holds names like CAMP_NOU;
  the folder is `st<id:03d>`.
- *"PES's bounce is a restitution coefficient."* Killed: it is
  `vy_out = -F*min(-imp, vy)`, which is reflection or restitution depending on
  landing speed.

### Performance (#87) and the speckled pitch (#89) — one cause

Every headless run this fork ever recorded went through **llvmpipe**: `xvfb-run`
hands the engine an X display with no GPU behind it. 32 fps in software; on the
card the engine saturates the 60 fps recorder (3,599 of 3,600 distinct frames
in a 60 s window). The red-mauve speckle over the pitch was the same thing —
precision noise from the software rasteriser that PES's colour table amplified
(39,781 distinct colours and 1.89% red-dominant pixels on llvmpipe, 2,002 and
0.00% on the GPU, same patch, same build). `tools/showcase.sh` now renders
through SDL's offscreen driver on `/dev/dri/renderD128`.

Load: 28.9 s → 18.6 s to gameplay. Stoppage cutscene clips (600 MB of text)
parse at the cut instead of at launch (`Match::CutsceneClip`); the animation
collection parses on every core. A first attribution by `ts` on a
block-buffered log was wrong and is withdrawn — it stamps flushes. `stdbuf -oL`.

**Killed:** an animation cache. The `.ase` cache that exists hashes 1.41 GB of
source on every launch to validate itself (0.94 s). The parser's cost is per
token and structural; parallelism took it from 8 s to 2.8 s with no on-disk state.

### gfviewer plays cutscenes

    gfviewer --cutscene <pack.chor> [--camtrack T] [--body M] [--authored-camera] --shots N --out DIR

Every slot cast on a skinned body through `EntranceChoreo::Sample` (the match's
own call), camera by montage timeline (`SampleTimeline`), re-aimed at the primary
actor with the match's 0.15 m guard unless `--authored-camera`. Three fixes to
get a second actor: unique clone postfix per body (shared copy → crash in
`Prepare`), `Exit()` in the destructor (abort at teardown), 40 ms pacing (the
60 Hz recorder was sampling 3 frames of 16), and the clip frame wrapped exactly
as `Humanoid::SetChoreoPose` wraps it.

## Diagnosed, not yet fixed

### Goal celebrations: the second part

PES's goal choreography is a **cast performance**: 406 of 534 `.chor`s have
2–11 actors (`_sub1..` clips with `phase` offsets), the primary's root track
walks him — 10 m over 16 s on `goal_2019_S_Golazo_00_overBoard_00` — and the
camera is a montage cutting through it. The match plays the scorer's clip via
`specialVar` **on the spot** (root motion baked out), re-aims the camera at
him, casts no teammates, and stops at `GoalSequence::CelebrationLength_ms`
(9 s of a 16 s performance). Everything after the individual bit — the run to
the board, the teammates arriving, the later cuts — is the missing second part.

**Plan:** play goals through the cutscene cast machinery
(`StartCutsceneChoreo` / `UpdateCutsceneChoreo` / `CutsceneClip`) with the
chosen celebration's own `.chor`: scorer = primary, teammates = extras, staged
at `goalCelebrationSubject` *and rotated by `goalCelebrationYaw`* (the stoppage
path only translates; the goal camera is yawed, so the cast must be too),
camera by `SampleTimeline` as now, length = the slowest slot's `phase + cycle`.
Retire the `specialVar` scorer path for filmed celebrations. Verify with the
viewer first (`--cutscene` on the same `.chor`), then a recorded goal.

### Actors lying flat

In the viewer: **all** thirty surveyed were the viewer reading past the clip's
end; wrapped, every one stands, coaches included. The clips' roots are near
identity in lying and upright cases alike — nothing is authored lying except
the genuine dives (`celebrate_0057`). In the match no cast path reads off the
end (`SetChoreoPose` wraps). **What is left:** a dive clip played on the spot
with its root motion baked out — a dive-and-roll becomes a man lying down where
he stood. Needs a match frame of it; ask for the celebration name from the
log line `celebration: <name> (var N)`.

### Camera cuts for foul, injury, offside

Not a cut-table problem. PES21 ships **two** foul cameras for 78 packs — one
static 6 s card-hold shot reused for red and yellow — and **no** offside camera
in any generation (`docs/PES_CUTSCENE_ARCHAEOLOGY.md` §3–4). Everything else
about a foul in PES is the live broadcast camera plus the replay system:
spec §3.2 has replay wipe → fence-view replay with the referee jogging in →
**push-in to a referee close-up held 5–6 s** → wipe out. The engine plays the
one card-hold shot for every foul subpool and a two-beat broadcast hold for
offside. **Plan:** record one of each (`debug_cutscene_report`), lay the beats
against §3.2's timings, and give the camera-less foul subpools the referee
push-in rather than the card hold. Stoppage tracks that *are* montages
(`timeup` 9/9, `result` 63/65, `end` 30/140, `change` 12/78) are sampled by
row in `Match::UpdateIngameCamera` (line ~3321) — `Sample`, which the goal
path's own comment says dissolves through cut points; switch to
`SampleTimeline` and verify a `timeup` on screen.

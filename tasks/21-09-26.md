# 21-09-26

## The kit slot was baked into the mesh; it is the engine's now

PES's "kit switcher" is a mesh UV-mapped onto the shared kit map (named
`dummy_kit`, or `uXXXXp0` when the pack named its geometry after the map).
PES paints the active kit preset onto it at run time; the engine's version of
that slot is `kit_template.png`, which `HumanoidBase::SetKit` swaps out for
the team's kit (the ident string is the basename, `humanoidbase.cpp:166`).

Two bugs, both fixed today:

1. **The importer matched the placeholder by prefix, not name.**
   `is_placeholder_texture` matched anything starting with `dummy`, so the
   kit's own stand-ins (`dummy_nrm`, `dummy_srm` - stand-in normal and
   specular maps) and a group named plain `dummy` (hdg's face slot) were all
   routed into the kit slot. Now: `is_kit_slot_texture` matches the whole
   name `dummy_kit`; the prefix helper is back to placeholders only
   (`fmdl_to_fullbody.py`, tests in `test_material_plan.py`).
2. **The importer routed only the unresolved case.** A group whose own texture
   the pack *does* ship went straight to the copy on disk, bypassing the kit
   test - so a pack that drops its first kit in as `Common/dummy_kit.dds`
   (ink's copy differs from `Kit Textures/u0XXXp1.dds` by a mean of 0.1 of
   255, DXT round-trip noise) froze the mesh on kit 1. Now the resolved case
   goes through `resolved_group_texture`: the kit slot wins whatever the pack
   ships, and the kit slot is also left out of the texture export (the copy
   would just duplicate a kit texture nobody samples).

### The installed tree could not be re-imported, so it was repaired in place

The source packs for smbg/smg/ink/lcg were in `/tmp/aet` and /tmp was cleared
mid-session; Downloads carries only 2HUG, HDG and DBG, and none of those has
a kit-switcher mesh (scanned: 0 `dummy_kit` groups). But the defect is in the
installed `.ase` itself, so it needed no pack to fix:
`tools/pes21_import/repoint_kit_slot_installed.py` re-points every kit-slot
mesh's diffuse map at `kit_template.png`, by material *reference* (not texture
name), touching only the `*MAP_DIFFUSE` block. Idempotent, dry-runnable.

It moved **14 meshes across 13 models** (ink_2434, lcg_2706, lcg_2710,
smbg_2593, smg_2578/2579/2581 (x2)/2585/2586/2587/2588/2591/2593). Two were
already correct (smbg_2581's `fullbody_u0821p0`, smg_2583) and were left
alone. `dbg_2016`'s `*_u0778p0` geoms look kit-named but are the character's
own body parts (they carry their own art, not the kit map) and were correctly
not touched - the repair decides by material, and their material is not the
kit slot. The `.geomcache` keys off an FNV-1a hash of the ASE bytes, so the
edit invalidates it on its own.

### Proof, in frames

`gfviewer` gained `--kit <file>`, which does exactly what
`Team::SetKitNumber` does in a match (swap every material whose ident is
`kit_template.png`, `OnUpdateGeometryData` after).

- `smbg_2593` (Bowser) with his team's kit: **the shorts now show the team
  crest** - before the fix that mesh was a blank corner of the body atlas.
- `lcg_2710` (composite import: 5 kit meshes) with the team kit
  `databases/default/images_teams/lcg/lcg_kit_01.png`: the red
  "Lobotomy Corporation" shirt, sleeves and socks are the team kit, and the
  head/shark/hair geoms keep their own art.

Both are whole figures with nothing floating or torn.

### Open, and where they came from

- `lcg_2710/lcg_2710_kit.png` (the per-model file `install_kit_texture`
  writes) is a **number strip, not a kit** - the pack's `Kit Textures`
  folder shipped something wrong, and the `p1`-prefers rule picked it. The
  engine never samples that file for a kit-slot mesh anymore, so it is
  harmless in matches; it is the wrong frame to hand a viewer.
- The smbg/smg/ink/lcg packs are gone from disk. Any future re-import of
  those characters needs them re-fetched; the repair script exists because of
  exactly this.
- UV animation (uvscroll/uvstep) is the next open line; not started.

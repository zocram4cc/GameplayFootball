in the tails), **8-14 shots a team depending on how aggressive the tactic is**,
**a touch fewer shots and especially fewer bad ones**, **mouth-save ~65%**.

Distribution went from 6-15 with nothing under 6, to median 4.5, range 1-8,

**Goals fell because arriving volume halved, not because the keeper improved.**
Mouth-save moved four points. Say so plainly whenever this table is quoted.

## What is settled, and what it cost to settle

1. **A seed does not reproduce a match.** Seed 5000 twice, sequentially, one
   engine, same binary and database and a byte-identical config: `1-5` and
   `0-2`. Real-time-paced stages diverge on wall-clock jitter alone.
   `HARNESSES.md` and `simbatch.sh` both claimed the opposite and are
   corrected (`c36934f`). **Per-seed deltas are noise; compare aggregates over
   a batch, both arms at equal concurrency.** An afternoon went on auditing a
   side that "collapsed from 13 shots to 0" on matched seeds.
2. **Every keeper in the database was a placeholder.** None of the 14 carried
   a single `gk_*`; all ran on outfield fallbacks at coverage 0.45. `gk_*`
   entered the importer on 09-06 and the database was last built 09-02.
   Fixed by `refresh_stats.py` (`a97f29f`), which writes stats in place
   matched by `(team, formationorder)` so row ids - and the model bindings
   keyed on them - never move.
3. **The importer fielded the wrong eleven.** `formationorder` came from the
   export's list order; PES's sheet is in `squad_order`, one slot per player,
   1-11 for the starters. Settled against all seven VGL26 exports - the only
   reading giving exactly eleven distinct-slot starters on every one
   (`4d39adb`). Slot is sheet ORDER, not position, so the keeper is pinned to
   seat 0 and the sheet fills the outfield ten.
4. **The keeper is beaten out of POSITION, not out of reach.** The census
   added this session reports, per match, whether he was beaten inside his own
   reaction or with time to move, and by how far. First clean match:
   `out of time 3-2 | out of reach 14-1 | mean gap 5.21m-7.50m`. No dive
   covers five metres. Two earlier keeper packages were fitted to reach
   constants and could never have shown up in a match; the save lever is
   `goalie_default.cpp` positioning.

## Open, in order

- [ ] **Positions, not just selection.** The sheet gives the eleven and their
  order; roles still come from the hardcoded `FIELD_ROLES` pattern, so
  /smbg/'s 0.97 (a CB in the source) is seated LM. PES's positions are the
  formation `slots` codes (`0` GK, then position ids), already decoded by
  `ted.read_formations` and currently unused. Map them, then re-measure.
- [ ] **Re-run the paired batch on the reseated squads.** Everything measured
  this week sits on top of teams fielding the wrong eleven. `tac12` was killed
  at 4/12 for exactly this. Nothing about shots or saves should be tuned until
  a batch is read against correct sides.
- [ ] **Shots to 8-14 by tactic.** `GetTacticalShotAppetite` is in and wired
  through `GetLiveTacticReal` (`e4e6b1e`), so philosophy, touchline
  instructions and a manager chasing a game all reach the trigger. Unmeasured:
  its only batch was the one killed.
- [ ] **Mouth-save 55.7% -> 65%, via positioning.** See finding 4. Do not
  touch `GetKeeperDiveReach_m` or `GetKeeperDiveSpeed_ms` without a census
  that says reach is what is short.
- [ ] **Fewer bad shots.** 20% of shots come from 18-25 m, 1% beyond. A bar of
  0.22 was tried inside the killed batch and never read.
- [ ] Pass accuracy 64-66% against the ~80% target. Untouched this session.

## Traps this session paid for

- **The showcase config has no shot bar.** `menu_smoke_showcase.config`
  carries no `gameplay_min_shot_xg`, so a showcase runs at the stock 0.04 and
  shows 14-73 shots, not the tuned balance. It cost one wrong diagnosis (a
  smoke read as "the spread tripled shot volume") before it was spotted.
- **`/dbg/` v `/smbg/` is a lopsided fixture.** /dbg/'s medals are benched in
  PES's own sheet, so its XI is 0.683 against /smbg/'s 0.768. It is a poor
  balance benchmark and its numbers should never be read as a balanced game.
- **Verify the instrument before believing it.** Three separate wrong turns
  this session came from a measurement, not the engine: `aimNoise` read as
  metres when `NormalizedClamp` returns 0..1; an audit check that ignored a
  column and invented three violations; a keeper census that sampled
  clearances half a pitch wide. Each looked like an engine defect first.
- **Never rewire on a half-understood field.** `squad_order` took four
  readings before one held across all seven exports. The first two looked
  right on the teams that happened to be near-identity.

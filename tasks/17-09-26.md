## Done today

- **Panic clearances were being counted as shots.** `_AddPanicPass` offers the
  hoof as three animations - high pass, long pass and `e_FunctionType_Shot`
  (`elizacontroller.cpp:1519`) - and whenever the picker took the shot variant,
  `humanoid.cpp` ran the whole shot branch on it: `AddShot`, the xG tally, the
  on-target projection, the save window. Measured on seed 5000: one side had
  7 "shots" at a **mean distance of 54 m**, five of them "wide". Both numbers
  the owner asked to move were contaminated, worst for the side under pressure.
  The command already carried `touchInfo.isClearance`; the shot branch now books
  it as a clearance, exactly as the pass branch already did.
- **`[balance-shots]` telemetry**: distance bands (inside 11 m / rest of the box
  / just outside / long range), mean distance, and the miss split (wide, over,
  short - a ball that meets the line plane below ground). Always on, because the
  sim runs a Release build where the `#ifndef NDEBUG` cards are compiled out.
  It rides in on the batch runner's existing `^\[balance` grep.
- **The cross-body slice is now the finisher's** (`GetShotBodySliceShare`).
  Striking across the body swings the ball off the boot by up to a quarter
  radian and spins it further the same way - the largest deterministic source of
  shots that miss the frame, and the only term in the strike that never asked
  who was taking it. A 0.99 finisher keeps 16% of the swing, a defender all of
  it. Floor 0.15: nobody squares the boot completely.
- **The shot bar is config-tunable** (`gameplay_min_shot_xg`, default the
  existing 0.04), so where chances are taken from can be measured without a
  rebuild.
- Worktrees pruned: `animreview`, `cutscenes`, `presentation`, `presentation2`,
  `rigdio`, `statsexport`, `trap`, `tri-c`, `engineopt` (its uncommitted
  phase-probe work saved to `/tmp/gpf-engineopt-uncommitted.patch`). 7 GB.

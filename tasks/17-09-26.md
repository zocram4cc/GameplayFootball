     nothing at any tier** - `AI_GetShotDirection` picks
     `goalHalfWidth - inset` whenever `autoDirectionBias >= 1.0` and DISCARDS

     sacrificed to reach them.** An unconstrained fit found a 0.48 m standing
     frame, which is not a human's arms and would have largely undone
     `19c0c4b`; holding the frame at 0.9 m and re-solving:

       `kKeeperStandingReach_m = 0.9 m` (flat, body geometry)

       placement bias ~0.5

     not from a crippled weak keeper.

     NOT APPLIED YET, deliberately: at the placement the engine produces (bias
     1.0) no tier saves anything, so the constants alone change nothing
     measurable. Placement and constants are one package.

     **How much placement error is needed, and how much exists.** The
     controller's `aimNoise` is `(1 - technical_shot) *
     NormalizedClamp(goalDist * 32, 8, 26)`, and `NormalizedClamp` returns
     0..1 (`bluntmath.cpp:30`), NOT metres - so `goalDist * 32` converts the
     normalised distance back to metres and the outer call normalises again.
     The error is therefore 0 inside 8 m, and at most 1.0 m for a zero-skill
     player beyond 26 m: **a 0.72 finisher gets at most +-0.28 m, a 0.99
     finisher +-0.01 m.** Reviving it is a straight revert, not a repair - and
     the value it revives is still far too small, because the corner gap it has
     to bracket is metres wide (the anchors' bias-0.5 fit is what ~half the
     placement distribution landing off the corner costs). So the anchors need
     a genuinely LARGER placement error than the engine has ever had, which is
     a design decision, and that is the decision outstanding.

## Custom models kept coming unbound: row ids were never stable (fix)

Owner: "STILL no custom models even in your tests. Why do they always get
unbound?" Then: "don't match by name; keep IDs the same on the GF side; ID is
the source of truth; names can change mid championship."

- Mechanism: `install_team.install` deleted a squad and re-inserted it with
  SQLite autoincrement ids, so every (re)import renumbered the roster. The
  three cfg files (`playermodels`, `playerportraits`, `playercelebrations`)
  key on `players.id`. A dead id is **silent**: `GetPlayerModelDir` returns
  empty and `Team::ActivateWithModel` fields the shared body with no warning
  (the warning only fires for a bound id whose files are missing). Every past
  "rebind" was an inline throwaway against whichever generation was on disk.
- Trigger this time, mine: I `git checkout`-ed `database.sqlite` treating it
  as ambient. It is not one of the owner's four files - it is generated state
  (refresh/reseat/reimports) - and the checkout reverted it to an older
  generation. 0 of the 46 dbg/smbg bindings then existed. Also destroyed the
  real keeper stats + reseated XIs; restored by `refresh_stats.py --reseat`.
  **Never checkout/revert database.sqlite; back it up and regenerate.**
- **The reason it recurred in every test, independent of the above:** the
  POST_BUILD step *copied* `data/databases` into `build/` but *symlinked*
  `media/`. The engine picks the first of `[cwd, exeDir, cwd/data,
  exeDir/data]` holding the database, and `tools/showcase.sh` runs from the
  repo root, so matches read `build/`'s build-time snapshot of the database
  against the live cfgs. Any db change after a build (refresh, reseat,
  reimport, rekey) = ids diverge = stock bodies. Proven: after the rekey, the
  first capture still loaded 0 custom models (build copy md5 580f30ad, dbg
  748..770; live d063fbb1, dbg 79001..). Fix: `databases/` linked with
  `cmake/link_media.cmake` like `media/`/`music/` (CMakeLists.txt). `build/`
  converted; build-asan/-debug/-full/-release/-tsan still hold copies and
  convert on their next build.
- Fix (TDD, 148/148): `install_team` writes `players.id = the export's own
  player id` (ted record id: 2hug 78301+, dbg 79001+, hdg 80301+, ink 80601+,
  lcg 80901+, smbg 82101+, vn 82901+). A reimport rewrites the same ids, so
  bindings can no longer strand. Two old tests that pinned "reinstall moves
  every id" rewritten to pin stability; a shadowed duplicate test class
  (never ran) deleted.
- Migration: `tools/pes21_import/rekey_player_ids.py` re-keys installed
  squads to export ids (positional, refuses on count or row-order drift,

- cfg repair: the committed cfgs referenced a vanished generation (ids
  817-908). Every generation restarted from the committed db's max id and

## Code review of everything since the two showcases (24-09)

Reviewed each commit after `/tmp/dbg_smbg_namek.mp4` and
`/tmp/showcase_2309.mp4`. Three real defects found and fixed; one item was
never implemented (GK), recorded as such rather than as a fix.

- **Goal scenes skipped the whole conceding side** (fixed). PES numbers a
  goal scene's actors 0-10 for the side the goal counts for and 11-21 for
  the side that conceded: `goal_2019_ins_lostGK_*` puts `gk_lost` on 12,
  `goal_S_owngoal_01_crouching` puts the own-goal scorer on 12. Every mark
  past 10 was skipped as a foul cutscene's "opponent", so the own-goal
  croucher was never posed and the unlucky man played the credited side's
  reaction. `GoalCelebration::OnConcedingSide`/`ScorerSlot` (pure, 4 new
  tests) decide the side; `StartGoalCast` casts each mark from it. Capture
  after: `7 of 7 marks cast` (was 5 of 7), cast upright, no latched bodies.
- **A released performer was reset every tick** (fixed). The play-once
  release ran `ProcessChoreo` + `ResetSituation` on every tick of the rest
  of the beat, and `ResetPosition` picks a *random* idle frame and clears
  the mental image each time. Since f744345 made every goal slot play
  once, that was every performer, for 4-9 s. One `released` flag per cast
  member; the reset runs once.
- **Penalty shootout lob (fixed, `01ecc92`)** - the arrival band was
  measured against the wrong goal, and the band was the thing throwing the
  ball over the bar.
  - Symptom: 3 of 7 kicks crossed the line at z 3.5-5.4 m against a 2.44 m
    bar and were recorded MISSED, two of them rolled as GOAL. The strike
    was *logged* flat (`z/|v|` = 0.05) and working kicks left at ~1.9 m/s
    vertical; the failing ones at **14.05 m/s**, a 25 deg launch. The
    classifier was honest - the ball really was lobbed, crossing at z 4.35.
  - Cause: `humanoid.cpp` measured the flight with `goalLineX =
    pitchHalfW * -team->GetSide()`. A shootout has both teams at ONE end
    (`GetGoalX` is the shootout's, not the taker's) and `GetSide()` is -1
    for team 0 and +1 for team 1, so **half the takers measured to the far
    goal: 96.6 m instead of 11.0 m**. `ShotArrivalVelocityZ` solves
    `h = z0 + vz*t - g*t^2/2`, so the cap grows with flight time - a 2.2 s
    flight turned the "stay under the bar" clamp into 11.0-12.5 m/s.
  - Killed first by measurement, not by reading: Magnus (0.03 m/s per
    tick; its `rotVec` is a *wrapped orientation angle*, not an angular
    velocity) and the worst-case blend (`z/|v|` 0.047). Neither explains
    14 m/s. Two arithmetic slips of my own, caught by the failing test:
    the near cap is 9.04 not <8, and the arrival identity already solves
    the gravity term - subtracting it twice is wrong.
  - Fix and evidence: ask the running shootout which end is in use. Before,
    `distGoal` read 96.6 / 102.0 / 96.1 m on the lobbed kicks against
    11.0-11.3 m on the scoring ones. After: **11.01-11.34 m on all nine**,
    band 2.0-8.9 m/s, all nine crossing inside the frame at z 0.11-1.25 m
    with no lobbed miss. The two remaining `[MISMATCH]` lines are the
    legitimate kind - a keeper rolled a SAVED kick wide enough to go in.
    Test: `AMeasuredFlightTimeFromTheFarGoalPermitsALob` pins that the
    near-end cap is a shot and the far-end cap is a lob.

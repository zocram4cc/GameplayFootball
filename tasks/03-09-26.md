# 03 September 2026 - goal sequence, penalty netting, the animation eye pass

## Fixed: goal -> celebration -> replay stopped firing, celebration repeated

Cause, one for both symptoms: the goal cast took its length from the .chor's
`cycleFrames` (`GetLastFrame`), which is PES's whole camera beat, not the
performance. Measured on `goal_celebrate_0057_base`: performance 10.19 s,
beat 26.95 s, capped by `kLongestCelebration_ms` to 20 s. So
  * the clips repeated to fill the 20 s window (`SetChoreoPose` wraps the
    frame, which is right for a walk-on marking time and wrong for a
    performance), and
  * the referee, which schedules the restart the tick after the goal off the
    *default* 9 s, called `ResetSituation` at 10.5 s and cleared the goal state
    the replay trigger waits on. No replay, ever.

Fix (`match.cpp`, `goalsequence.*`): the cast's length is the last actor's clip
ending; an actor who is done holds his last pose; `RestartPrepareAt_ms` takes
the celebration length and Match pushes the referee's buffer back once the clip
is chosen (`AlterSetPiecePrepareTime`). `ScheduleIsConsistent` and a new test
assert the order for every length up to the cap.
Verified in `/tmp/seqcheck.mp4` + `.log`: both goals log
"performance runs 10190 ms of a 26950 ms beat" and "goal replay: celebration
ran 10190 ms, replay reaches 20190 ms back"; on screen goal -> celebration ->
wipe -> replay -> wipe -> kickoff.

## Fixed: a scored penalty flew through the net

`Match::SetGoalScored(false)` also cleared `ballIsInGoal`, and the netting in
`Ball::Process` is gated on that flag. The shootout calls it the moment it sees
the ball cross, i.e. 1-4 ticks after the ball entered - so the netting switched
off with the ball still doing 25 m/s inside the goal, and it left the stadium.
Netting is now only cleared by a reset. Measured over a shootout
(`/tmp/penprobe.log`, ball position logged every tick): 7 of 7 scored penalties
now stop at 57.67-57.95 m against a rear panel at 57.66 m, one of them struck
at 38 m/s. `ball_physics_test.cpp` gained the netting's first two tests.

Open, same report: a penalty *aimed wide* crosses the byline outside the posts
(measured y=6.1 m) and there is nothing behind the goal - no net, no stand - so
it rolls 28 m out (x=-83.6) with the camera following it off the pitch. Tried
killing the ball's momentum in `FinishKick`: wrong, because the verdict lands
the moment the ball crosses, so goals froze at the line (max |x| 55.78 instead
of 57.8). Reverted. The real fix is perimeter geometry behind the goal.

## Performance pass 1: 36 -> 76 fps on this machine (2.1x headroom)

Measured on the same fixture throughout - dbg v smbg at st017, Release
(`-O3 -DNDEBUG`), headless on the card, medians of 5-second windows over
several 95-second runs with nothing else on the machine. Early numbers in this
file that disagree were taken with `perf record` attached or with a second
engine running; those are void.

|state|frame|fps|
|---|---|---|
|before|27.8 ms|36.0|
|+ vertex copy: by-reference, changed-attributes-only|22.4 ms|44.6|
|+ distant bodies at half rate (now the default)|18.1 ms|55.2|
|+ partial vertex-buffer upload (dynamic prefix only)|13.8 ms|72.5|
|+ flat per-vertex weight table|13.2 ms|75.9|

What each one was:
* `Geometry::OnUpdateGeometry` copied `GeometryData::triangleMeshes` **by
  value**, deep-copying every submesh's index vector every frame (~14 MB and 84
  allocations a frame over 28 bodies), then memcpy'd all five attribute arrays
  into the vertex buffer although skinning only rewrites positions and normals.
  Both fixed: a reference, and a per-GeometryData mask of which elements are
  dynamic (`SetDynamicElements`, set by `PrepareFullbodyModel` - tangents only
  when a normal map is bound, texture vertices never). 2.7 GB/s -> 1.1 GB/s.
* The GL thread then orphaned the whole buffer and memcpy'd all of it again.
  `UpdateVertexBuffer` now takes a `dynamicFloats` prefix: the layout is
  element-major, so positions+normals are the first two fifths, and both
  ping-pong slots already hold the static tail from `CreateVertexBuffer`.
* `animation_halve_distant_bodies` defaults to true (see the code comment).
* `WeightedVertex` held a `std::vector<WeightedBone>` per vertex - a heap chase
  per vertex over ~1M vertices a frame. Flat `jointID[4]/weight[4]` with a
  count; `vertexID` deleted, it was always the loop index.
* The four `Skinning` kernels and `Vector3::FastNormalize`/`GetDotProduct` are
  `inline` in their headers: the build has no LTO, so out of line they were
  five to eight real calls per vertex (PlayerRuntime's objdump).
* `Animation::GetInterpolatedValues` walked the key map from `begin()` and
  pushed every passed key into a fresh vector; now `lower_bound` + `std::prev`.
  1,599 of 1,892 installed clips are keyed on every frame, and this runs twice
  per node per put and once per frame of every clip at load.

Verified: 1,381 tests pass, and a 1-minute-halves capture renders players with
correct textures and no torn geometry (/tmp/pc_zoom.png), which is what the
partial upload had to be checked against.

### The remaining ladder, for the next pass
`UpdateFullbodyModel` is now 45.7% of self time on its own and the memmove is
down to ~19%. In expected order of value for a weak machine:
 1. Skin straight into the vertex buffer. The middle copy (mesh array ->
    concatenated buffer) only exists to concatenate; ~19% of the frame.
 2. Mesh LOD. The imported bodies are 20k-100k+ vertices with no LOD at all;
    a decimated mesh for anything past ~20 m is the biggest single lever left
    and it is importer work, not engine work.
 3. Off-camera culling: bodies behind the camera are skinned in full. Needs the
    camera basis on the game thread; getting the forward sign wrong would skip
    visible bodies, so it wants a frame to check against.
 4. Skip normal skinning for distant bodies (shading only), ~40% of their
    per-vertex cost.
 5. SIMD: the loop is scalar. `-march=x86-64-v2` or a dispatched AVX2 path for
    the skinning translation unit only - but v3/AVX2 is a 2013 floor, which is
    a distribution decision rather than a free win.

## Fixed: the offside restart, and cutscenes posing players in live play

Two causes, both from MatchPresentation's review, both matching the owner's
report from showdown2.mp4 at 04:42 / clock 33:03 (THE CHAMP! standing on the
ball, keeper kicking into him):

* The offside kept upstream's schedule - prepare at +2 s, take at +4 s - while
  starting a 7 s choreography over the top, so `ResetSituation` teleported
  everyone into their restart positions two seconds in and the whistle went at
  four, with three seconds of staging still to run. `SetPieceLaws` now owns the
  offside timings (`kOffsideCutscene_ms`, `OffsidePrepareAt_ms`,
  `OffsideTakeAt_ms`) and `StartCutscene` takes its length from the same
  constant, so the two cannot drift. Two tests assert the order.
* `Match::UpdateCutsceneChoreo` fed its cast for as long as its own clock ran,
  whatever the match was doing. A posed player is fed every tick and
  `HumanoidBase::Process` returns early while he is, so he stood frozen in live
  play - and being the man nearest the ball he was also the taker everyone was
  waiting for. A stoppage's cast is now released the moment `IsInPlay()`.
* `cutsceneEnd_ms` was on the wall clock while its only reader (the goal replay
  trigger) compares against the match clock, so after any cutscene the trigger
  waited on a moment that never came and the goal replay was silently lost.
  Now `actualTime_ms + length`.

Verified on /tmp/offcheck.mp4: the offside at clock 9:37 (video 60 s) plays
flag and complaint through, then the restart, then play - nobody frozen over
the ball.
